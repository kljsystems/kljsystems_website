Questions, answered

Do you charge to look at my computer?

The diagnostic is $49. If you go ahead with the repair, that $49 comes off the bill. You get the price in writing before we touch anything.

Do I have to bring it in?

No. Drop it off, book a local pickup, or let us connect remotely; most software problems (slowdowns, viruses, setup) can be fixed without the machine leaving your desk.

Can you build with parts I already own?

Yes. We check what you have, keep what is still worth keeping, and quote only for the rest. Upgrades to an existing PC work the same way.

What does AI automation mean for a small business?

Taking one task you repeat every day and handing it to software: sorting email, pulling numbers out of invoices, answering the same customer questions, booking appointments. We start with one task, measure the time saved, then decide what is next.

Will I be able to update my own website?

Yes. Every site comes with a short walkthrough of how to change text, photos and opening hours. If you would rather not, the care plan covers updates for you.

Are my files safe during a repair?

We back up your data before any risky work when the drive allows it, we do not browse your files, and anything we recover stays with you.

Tell us what you need

A broken laptop, a new website or a task that eats your week: send a few lines and get a reply within one business day.
