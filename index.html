Here's how a job goes.

Every job, from a cracked screen to a new website, goes the same way. You'll always know what's happening and what it costs.

  1. Tell us what's wrong, or what you want.

    Use the form, call, or drop in. Plain words are fine; "it makes a clicking noise and then turns off" tells us plenty.

  2. Get the price in writing.

    We diagnose or scope it, then send a fixed quote. Nothing is ordered and no work starts until you reply yes.

  3. Pick it up working.

    Repairs usually take 1 to 3 days, builds 5 to 10. We show you what we did, and you can message us afterwards.

What people ask us first.

Do you charge to look at my computer?

The diagnostic is $49. If you go ahead with the repair, that $49 comes off the bill. You'll have the price in writing before we touch anything.

Do I have to bring it in?

No. Drop it off, book a local pickup, or let us connect remotely. Most software problems (slowdowns, viruses, setup) can be fixed without the machine leaving your desk.

Can you build with parts I already own?

Yes. We check what you have, keep what's still worth keeping, and quote only for the rest. Upgrades to an existing PC work the same way.

What does AI automation mean for a small business?

Taking one task you repeat every day and handing it to software: sorting email, pulling numbers out of invoices, answering the same customer questions, booking appointments. We start with one task, measure the time saved, then decide what's next.

Will I be able to update my own website?

Yes. Every site comes with a short walkthrough of how to change text, photos and opening hours. If you'd rather not, the care plan covers updates for you.

Are my files safe during a repair?

We back up your data before any risky work when the drive allows it, we don't browse your files, and anything we recover stays with you.

Tell us what's broken.

Or what you'd like built. A laptop that won't start, a site you've put off for a year, a task that eats your Monday. Send a few lines and you'll hear back within one business day.
